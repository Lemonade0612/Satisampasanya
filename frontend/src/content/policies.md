# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 12 November 2026

Sati ("Sati", "we", "us") is a bilingual Lao/English platform that helps people in Laos recognise and avoid online scams and human trafficking. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. We collect as little as we can, and we never sell your data.

## 1.1 Who runs Sati

Sati is run by Team Satisampasanya, Vientiane, Lao PDR. Contact: satisampasanya@gmail.com.

## 1.2 What we collect

**Information you give us**

- **Account details:** display name, email address and password (stored encrypted, never readable by us).
- **Age band:** Kids (6–14), Youth (15–24), Adults (25–59) or Elders (60+). We ask for an age band, not your date of birth, so we can show content suitable for you.
- **Language choice:** Lao or English.
- **Posts and reports:** what you post, comment on or report to Sati, including screenshots or links you share.
- **AI chat messages** (Youth and older only): the questions you ask the Sati AI assistant.
- **Messages to us:** anything you send us for support or feedback.

**Information collected automatically**

- **Game and learning activity:** which games and scenarios you play and your scores, so we can track progress and improve lessons.
- **Basic technical data:** device type, operating system, app version, approximate time zone, and error logs, so the app works and stays secure.

**What we do not collect:** precise location, contacts, your phone's photos (unless you choose to upload one), government ID numbers, or bank details. Sati will never ask for your password, OTP code or money.

## 1.3 How we use it

- To run your account and show content, games and AI chat suited to your age band and language.
- To check posts for scams, trafficking risk, bullying, profanity and off-topic content (see 1.5).
- To let verified experts review reported posts and mark them green, yellow or red.
- To keep Sati safe: detect abuse, spam and fake accounts.
- To improve Sati using **anonymised, combined statistics** (for example, "how many job-scam posts were reported this month"). Published impact numbers never identify individuals.

## 1.4 Who we share it with

We share data only as needed to run Sati:

- **Service providers** that host and power the app: our hosting provider (the server and database that run Sati) and OpenAI (the AI behind Sati's Lao/English translation, post screening and assistant). They process data only on our instructions. We send requests to OpenAI with storage turned off, and under OpenAI's API terms this data is not used to train its models by default.
- **Verified experts and moderators** who review reported posts. They see the post and report, not your login details.
- **Authorities**, only when required by Lao law, or when we believe someone is in immediate danger (for example, an active trafficking situation).

Some providers store data on servers outside Laos. We choose providers with strong security protections.

## 1.5 AI and automated moderation

Sati uses AI to (a) translate posts between Lao and English, (b) answer safety questions in the chat and (c) check new posts before or after they appear. AI can be wrong. A post flagged by AI can be reviewed by a human expert, and you can ask for a review of any decision about your post (see 2.5). AI answers are for education only and are not legal, financial or emergency advice.

## 1.6 Children (Kids band, ages 6–14)

- Children under 15 should use Sati with a parent, guardian or teacher, who must agree to this policy on their behalf.
- Kids cannot use the AI chat and cannot post publicly. Their "posts" are simulated practice examples created by Sati.
- We collect only a display name, age band and game progress for Kids accounts. We do not show children any advertising.
- A parent or guardian can ask us to see or delete their child's data at any time: satisampasanya@gmail.com.

## 1.7 How long we keep data

- Account data: while your account is active, then deleted within 30 days of you deleting the account.
- Posts: until you or a moderator delete them. Removed scam or trafficking posts may be kept for up to 12 months as evidence and to train our checks, with personal details removed where possible.
- AI chat messages: 90 days, then deleted.
- Technical logs: up to 30 days.

## 1.8 Your choices and rights

You can: see the data we hold about you; correct it; download it; delete your posts or your whole account (email us at satisampasanya@gmail.com); and change your age band or language. We will reply within 30 days.

## 1.9 Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), encrypted passwords, access controls so only authorised admins can see admin data, and regular reviews of who has access. No system is perfect; if a data breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

## 1.10 Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we will notify you in the app before the change takes effect.

# Community Guidelines

Sati exists to help people spot scams and trafficking and to protect each other. These rules keep it safe, especially for young users.

## 2.1 What belongs on Sati

- Warnings about scams, fake job offers, romance scams, investment or gambling scams, and suspicious recruiters.
- Questions like "Is this message real?" with a screenshot (blur personal details first).
- Stories and lessons from your own experience.
- Safety tips and verified information.

## 2.2 Not allowed

- **Scams or recruitment:** posting real scam offers, job ads for unknown companies, loan offers, or links that ask for money, passwords or OTP codes.
- **Bullying and hate:** insults, threats, humiliation, or attacks on someone's ethnicity, religion, gender, disability or appearance.
- **Profanity:** swearing or vulgar content, including posts that are only profanity.
- **Sharing private information (doxxing):** phone numbers, addresses, ID cards or photos of other people without consent. If you report a scammer, share their scam message, not personal details of victims.
- **Political claims and misinformation:** political campaigning, unverified political claims, and false information presented as fact. Satire must be clearly labelled.
- **Off-topic content:** sales, ads, spam, chain messages, or posts unrelated to online safety.
- **Sexual or violent content**, and anything that sexualises or endangers children. We report child exploitation to the authorities.
- **Impersonation:** pretending to be an expert, official, police or another user.
- **Manipulated media** presented as real (edited screenshots, fake documents, deepfakes).

## 2.3 Post status colours

- 🟢 **Green — Verified:** checked by an expert and safe or accurate.
- 🟡 **Yellow — Under review / be careful:** not yet verified, or partly suspicious.
- 🔴 **Red — Scam / danger:** confirmed scam or risk. Do not contact, pay or click.

If an expert has not reviewed a post within 48 hours, it is reviewed by the AI fallback and stays labelled until an expert confirms it.

## 2.4 What happens if rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is: the post is hidden or removed; a warning; temporary posting limits; or a permanent ban. Real scam or trafficking activity may be reported to the authorities.

## 2.5 Reports and appeals

Tap **Report** on any post you think breaks these rules. If your post was removed and you think it was a mistake, tap **Ask for review** or email satisampasanya@gmail.com. A human will look at it.

## 2.6 If you or someone else is in danger

Sati is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger or being held against their will, contact the police (**1191**) immediately.
